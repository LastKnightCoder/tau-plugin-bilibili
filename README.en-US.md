# Bilibili

Add courses and lectures from Bilibili to Tau so you can take notes while watching. Videos are cached locally for offline playback; when subtitles are available, you can read along and clip lines straight into your notes.

## Get Started in 3 Steps

1. **Install the plugin**: Install "Bilibili" from the Plugin Center and enable it right away—no restart needed.
2. **Add a video**: Choose "Bilibili" when using "Add video" in a project or inserting a video in the editor or Whiteboards, then paste the video link.
3. **Choose quality**: Once parsed, pick a quality option. Playback begins as soon as the video downloads locally.

## What You Can Do

- **Take Video Notes**: Videos added to a project open in a split view—watch on the left and write on the right—with timestamps and frame snapshots. See [Video Notes](https://taunote.app/docs/video-notes/) for details.
- **Read along with subtitles**: Built-in subtitles display automatically and scroll with playback. If subtitles are missing or inaccurate, use transcription to convert speech to text.
- **Embed in notes and Whiteboards**: Insert videos directly into note bodies and Whiteboards alongside your other content.
- **Use as study material**: Add videos as sources in a Notebook so Tau can convert them to text for AI search and Q&A.

## Supported Links

- Video page links: BV IDs, av IDs, and multi-part links with `?p=`;
- `b23.tv` short links.

Licensed anime, paid courses, and other separately authorized content are not supported.

## Higher Video Quality

You can add videos up to 720P without signing in. For higher quality, open Settings on the plugin details page and click "Scan QR code" to sign in with the Bilibili mobile app, or choose "Enter manually" and paste `SESSDATA` from your browser cookies. Signing in unlocks 1080P, while 1080P+ and 4K require a Premium member account. Subtitles for some videos also require signing in.

Sign-in credentials are stored only on your computer, and the QR code is generated locally.

## FAQ

**Does it work on mobile?** No, the Bilibili plugin is desktop-only.

**What if it says the video stream is unavailable?** The video may require a Premium member account or may no longer be available. Try signing in with a Premium member account or selecting a different quality.
